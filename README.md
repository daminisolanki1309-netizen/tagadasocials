# tagadasocials

Scooby, the daily social research assistant for Tagada, TagadaPay and TagadaRx.

Every morning it reads about 200 global brands, founders and operators on X, LinkedIn and Instagram, keeps the posts beating their own numbers in the last 48 hours, finds the trends blowing up across social media, writes ready-to-edit swipes for all three brands and logs the run at the top of the Notion page **Scooby Runs**. Nothing goes to Slack or email.

- `HANDOFF.md`: current state, Notion IDs, scheduled tasks, blockers. Read this first in a new session.
- `CLAUDE.md`: how to answer "give me links" requests.
- `tagada/README.md`: how the engine works.
- `tagada/prompts/daily-swipe-drop.md`: the daily scheduled task's instructions.
- `tagada/latest-links.md`, `tagada/feed/`: today's feed and trends.
- `tagada/engine/`: the scanner, link checker and feed builder.
