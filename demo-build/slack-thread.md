Berry's got a sibling 🐶 Meet *Scooby*.

Scooby is a social research assistant I built for us. Every morning it finds the posts beating their own numbers on X, LinkedIn and Instagram, from brands and founders worldwide, and turns them into ready-to-edit swipes for Tagada, TagadaPay and TagadaRx.

1-minute demo below 👇 Details in the thread.


REPLY 1

🔎 *What Scooby does*
• Every morning at 8:45am IST it reads what brands and founders worldwide posted in the last 48 hours: 175 accounts (84 on X, 40 on LinkedIn, 51 on Instagram) plus the Meta Ad Library for our space. The list grows every day.
• It scores each post against that account's usual post, so we see what actually broke out, newest first. A link never shows up twice.
• It opens every link before sharing it, and saves the post link, the profile link and a screenshot. No dead links.
• It writes a swipe for every banger that fits Tagada, TagadaPay or TagadaRx, on X, LinkedIn and Instagram. No daily cap: Monday's first run found 8 bangers and wrote 10 swipes.
• When we post one, Scooby finds it on our account the next morning and tracks how it did against the post it was built on. Nobody has to type numbers in.


REPLY 2

💡 *How it helps us*
• We stop starting from a blank page. Every post starts from something that is working right now, with the numbers to show it.
• Design and copy reviews get easier because each draft comes with its reference.
• Monthly planning gets faster: paste the content calendar and every post comes back with references, design direction and draft copy.
• We finally see what works for us: the dashboard compares each swipe we post with its reference.
