## How the copy was written
- Benefit or the merchant's moment first, then the how. Shopify gets credit, never a dig.
- No em dashes, no exclamation points, straight quotes, US spelling.
- X posts are all under 280 characters.
- One idea per post where the brief asked for one.
## Before anything goes to review
- **Placeholders in \[square brackets\]:** team size, countries, cities, names, hiring roles, in-person cadence, what the team does to prepare for peak. Posts 6, 7, 8C, 16C/D, 17, 18, 22, 23, 24B/C.
- **Sensitivity field:** posts 10, 11B, 13 and 22 name Shopify and/or describe Tagada capabilities. The calendar template asks for Sensitivity to be set before Review.
- **Proof number for post 6:** the \$20M figure appears with two wordings (revenue vs raised). Confirm the exact phrase with Loïc.
- **Post 25:** the brief field is empty. Copy follows the title. Worth a quick check with Charlotte.
- **Feedback on the calendar's EXEMPLE 1** asked for copy that is "more humain," so the options lean casual on X and Instagram and stay measured on LinkedIn.
