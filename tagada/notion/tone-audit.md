## The short version
Tagada already has one strong voice: the operator who knows exactly where the money leaks. It shows up on the homepage and on TagadaPay. Every other channel dilutes it with jargon, generic hustle memes, blog filler and small slips. The best proof Tagada has (real merchant numbers) gets 0 to 8 likes because it is buried in long technical posts.

## Proposed voice
### In one sentence
**Tagada sounds like an operator who has had payouts frozen, fixed it, and now tells other founders exactly what to do next.**
The founders ran ecommerce brands. The voice should sound like the friend who already got the "payouts on hold" email and knows the next three steps. Calm, specific, a little dry. Never a lecture.
### The one rule: Benefit, then How, then Proof
1. **Benefit:** what the merchant gets, in their words (more orders approved, cash on time, fewer apps).
2. **How:** the feature that delivers it, in one plain sentence. The technical name can follow in brackets if needed.
3. **Proof:** a real number, a screenshot or a named review.
**Example**
- Today: "Cascade on decline… ship a native MID in 24 hours"
- Benefit first: "Save the sale when a card gets declined."
- How: "We retry it on a second processor in the background, and we can get you your own merchant account within 24 hours."
- Proof: "One merchant went from 72% to 84% approvals in six months."

### SEO, AEO and GEO checklist for every post
- **Answer first:** the first two sentences answer the title question in plain words. AI tools quote that part.
- **Question headings:** use the questions people type ("How long does a Shopify payout hold last?") as subheadings, each with a short direct answer underneath.
- **FAQ block plus FAQ markup:** 3 to 5 real questions at the end, marked up so Google and AI tools can read them.
- **A named author:** a real person with a short bio and a photo (a founder or the payments lead), plus author markup. AI tools and Google trust named experts more.
- **Original data:** at least one number only Tagada has (average approval lift, typical hold length). This is what gets Tagada cited in AI answers.
- **One post per search:** check the list above before writing so posts don't compete.
- **Internal links:** every post links to the main guide on its topic and to one product page.
- **Updated date:** show "Updated on" and refresh the top 20 posts each quarter.
- **Keep llms.txt current:** add new guides to it when they go live.
- **Vary the openings:** no more "Most advice..." starts.
