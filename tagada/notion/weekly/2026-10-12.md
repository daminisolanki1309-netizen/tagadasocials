### P-X2 · X thread · the Bencivenga proof play, built on [USA Payments' 163-day ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=2410258636085176)
```plain text
1/ We published our processor terms on our homepage. Here's every line merchants ask about, in plain English.

2/ Minimum volume: none. No monthly minimum, no minimum fee, and your rate doesn't depend on volume.

3/ Reserve: usually 10% of each sale, held 60 to 90 days, then released automatically. It can be lower or zero depending on your history.

4/ If chargebacks spike, our risk system can raise the reserve to 40%. Anything higher is decided by a person. Every change comes in writing, with the reason, and you get 30 days to ask for a review.

5/ Payouts: every day. A sale is payable 2 days after capture in the US.

6/ Disputes: $25 per dispute, $30 per chargeback alert.

7/ Suspension: a person decides, not an automated system, and we tell you what to fix and by when.

8/ Leaving: 30 days' written notice, either side.

9/ Ask your current processor for the same eight answers in writing. If they won't put them in writing, that is your answer.
```
- **Alt hooks (post 1):** "Most processors bury their reserve terms. Ours are on the homepage." / "The eight questions to ask any processor before you sign."
- **Design direction:** text thread. Attach a screenshot of the [terms block](https://www.tagadapay.io/#:~:text=The%20terms%20that%20decide%20it) to post 1.
- ⚠️ Every line is quoted from [tagadapay.io](https://www.tagadapay.io/) as of Oct 18. Do not post until the one-star reviews in Tone Audit 2b are answered; posts 4 and 7 will draw them as replies.
