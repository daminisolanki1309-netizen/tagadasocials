## How to read a weekly page
- **Outlier score** = that post's engagement divided by the account's typical post. 3 or more is worth studying. Engagement = likes + 2 × replies + 3 × reposts. Reels use views when Instagram shows them. Meta ads have no public engagement, so they are ranked by how long they have been running (an ad that runs for months is paying for itself).
- **Three links on every post:** the post, the account's profile, and a no-login view (the platform's own embed page) that opens even when X, Instagram or LinkedIn ask you to log in.
- **If a link breaks:** the screenshot and the saved post text inside the Swipe File row are the permanent copy. A Wayback Machine link is added when one exists.
