Research hub for Tagada's X, LinkedIn and Instagram. 
<callout icon="📏" color="gray_bg">
	**How outliers are scored.** For each account, the engine takes its recent original posts (no replies, no reposts) and finds the typical (median) post. Engagement = likes + 2 × replies + 3 × reposts. A post's **Outlier score** is its engagement divided by that typical post. 3 or more = an outlier worth studying. Instagram uses views instead of engagement.
</callout>
