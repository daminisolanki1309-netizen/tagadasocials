## Who counts as a competitor
**Direct competitors** sell the same thing to the same buyer: a checkout, funnel, subscription CRM or payment processor for DTC brands. Tagada itself names 4 of them with comparison pages: [vs Checkout Champ](https://www.tagada.io/vs/checkoutchamp), [vs sticky.io](https://www.tagada.io/vs/stickyio), [vs Funnelish](https://www.tagada.io/vs/funnelish) and [vs ClickFunnels](https://www.tagada.io/vs/clickfunnels). 
Two high-risk processors compete with TagadaPay: 
1. Easy Pay Direct 
2. PaymentCloud.
## In short
Nobody in this category is good at social. The accounts with big follower counts (ClickFunnels) are living off an old audience, and everyone else posts event photos or feature tips to a few hundred people. Every competitor also sells the same promise: everything in one place. The opening for Tagada is to be the one brand that talks about the merchant's money problem (holds, declines, shutdowns) in the merchant's words, backs it with real numbers, and shows up where buyers search and ask AI tools.

### 2. Everyone sells the same promise
- [sticky.io](http://sticky.io)'s long-running Meta ad: "Your revenue stack shouldn't be spread across disconnected tools." ([Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=US&q=sticky.io&search_type=keyword_unordered), running since Jul 15, 2026)
- [sticky.io](http://sticky.io) homepage: "run offers, subscriptions, payments, and recovery in one place" ([sticky.io](https://sticky.io))
- Checkout Champ, Funnelish and Tagada all lead with "all-in-one" too.
**What this means:** "Everything in one place" is now the category's default line, so it no longer sets Tagada apart. What only Tagada has is the processor and the platform together (TagadaPay plus the OS). Lead with what that does for the merchant: more orders approved and no shutdown that stops sales.

### 5. What their customers complain about
- **Slow support:** Funnelish, "You have to wait over 12 hours for a single response" (Will). PaymentCloud, "No one answers the phone" (Brooke).
- **Payments failing:** Easy Pay Direct, "Every week the processing of payments fail. I have customers calling me all the time saying they can't pay." (Ali)
- **Billing and price jumps:** [sticky.io](http://sticky.io), "They processed cancellation and STILL billed me \$1000 with NO services rendered." (pen jay). Capterra reviewers say [sticky.io](http://sticky.io) "tripled in price".
- **Fees that grow with you:** Checkout Champ on G2, "Cost - charge per order increases monthly fee".
The review pages are linked in the table above. These are quoted as reported by the review sites on Oct 5; open the review page before quoting any of them publicly.
**What this means:** the top 3 things buyers fear from this category are slow support, payments that fail, and surprise bills. Tagada can win all 3, but only if it fixes its own pricing contradiction first (Tone Audit, point 5). "A human answers fast", "leave in one click" (Loïc's "0% lock-in" post) and one clear price are the lines to repeat.

### 7. Where they put their content
- **Checkout Champ** runs long YouTube webinars aimed at Tagada's exact pain: ["How to Scale Through Black Friday 2025 Without Payment Shutdowns"](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC9dpyiDLKN06MxNUxXN2sDg/videos). Its best Instagram reel of the year (13.9K views against about 600 usual) is a contrarian hook: ["Your Favorite eCommerce Platform Doesn't Want You To Know About This..."](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbIxEl7A7dw/)
- **Eightx** wins through search and AI answers. Founder Matt Putra [posted](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mattputra/recent-activity/all/) that 10 days into an SEO and AI-search push, prospects called saying they found Eightx first in ChatGPT. It also has comparison pages against 7 rival CFO firms, like [Eightx vs Pilot](https://eightx.co/blog/compare/eightx-vs-pilot).
- **Funnelish** posts several tactical funnel tips a day on X to a small audience.
- **ClickFunnels** runs on founder fame and customer stories ("He Turned a Window Cleaning Business Into Millions"), plus a big Facebook group.
- [**sticky.io**](http://sticky.io)** and PaymentCloud** mostly post about trade shows.
## How Tagada beats them
### 5 solid moves
1. **Own the payment-shutdown moment.** No competitor posts about holds, freezes and declines in the merchant's words, and that is what gets Tagada's best results (Eden's suspension post: 305 likes, 478K views). Make it a weekly series on X, LinkedIn and Instagram.
2. **Put one provable number everywhere.** Pick the number (total processed, average approval lift, or money recovered), fix the homepage source, and put it in every bio and ad. Competitors' strongest ads are a single number.
3. **Get 30 real reviews in 60 days.** That puts Tagada ahead of every checkout competitor except ClickFunnels on Trustpilot. Replace the placeholder testimonials with them.
4. **Win search and AI answers before Black Friday.** Answer the questions merchants type ("Shopify payouts on hold", "high risk payment processor for supplements", "Checkout Champ alternative") with a named author and FAQ markup. Add comparison pages for the processors: vs Easy Pay Direct, vs PaymentCloud, vs Shopify Payments.
5. **Sell the outcome, not the price.** Fix the pricing contradiction, then compare cost per approved order and apps cancelled, not platform fee.

## Update Oct 6: what the paid ads show
- **Easy Pay Direct just went on the attack.** On Oct 5, 2026 it launched 2 Meta ads aimed at Stripe users: "there is a HIGH chance your merchant account could be shut down at any moment." [Ad 1](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1557844199341241), [Ad 2](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1948169262809446). Black Friday fear is now a paid message, not only a Checkout Champ webinar topic.
- **How Tagada answers it:** do not copy the fear. Lead with the calm benefit and the proof. "If one processor says no, your store keeps selling." Then show how: more than one processor, each order sent to the one that will approve it.
- **ClickFunnels sells with a deadline that never ends.** 28 of its top 30 ads have run 30+ days, most of them "YOUR LIFETIME CLICKFUNNELS OFFER HAS ALMOST EXPIRED" since March. [See the ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=953499030728257). Tagada can stand apart by being specific and calm.
- **Eightx runs few ads but keeps them on for months.** About 10 Meta ads, all 30+ days, oldest since Feb 11, 2026. Short founder-voice jabs like "Your ROAS looks great." [See the ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=2165392530950178). This is the closest model for Tagada's first paid test.
- **Stripe owns the "best payment method at checkout" message on Meta** since Mar 11, 2026. [See the ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=930488769493092). Tagada should not fight on that line; it should own "keep selling when a processor says no."
- **On Instagram, collabs win.** Wise (32x with a football podcast), Artisan (858K views with Jordan Belfort, on a 3.2K follower account), Gong (3.2x with a CEO podcast). A small account can borrow reach. All rows are in the Swipe File.
