**LI4. Visa moved the line** · Bencivenga urgent problem · Unaware to problem aware
```plain text
On April 1, Visa moved a line that most store owners have never heard of.

It's called the VAMP ratio: fraud reports and disputes added together, divided by your transactions. The "excessive" level for merchants in North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific dropped from 2.2% to 1.5%.

Cross it, and once you also pass 1,500 cases in a month, the fees and the hard conversations with your processor start.

Most founders I talk to know their ROAS to two decimals and can't tell me this number.

Before Black Friday doubles your transactions, it's worth knowing. Ask your processor for it this week. If they can't give it to you quickly, that tells you something too.
```
*Source: *[*Fraud.net*](https://fraud.net/resources/visas-vamp-thresholds-drop-to-1-5-on-april-1-are-you-ready)*. Payments team to check the wording.*
