**One thing the Swipe File warns about:** Notion's "Something new is coming" got 6.1x because millions already follow Notion. The same kind of teaser from a small account gets ignored. So the strongest options for Tagada give people something to do (guess, reply) or something specific to picture (four days, a circled date, a forecast).

## Before posting
- "Four days" means Black Friday to Cyber Monday (Nov 27 to Nov 30, 2026).
- C only works with a real prize (early access, a first look at the demo). Leave it out if there isn't one.
- E and H need the reveal day and the month work started.
- F says "six weeks to Black Friday", which is right if it goes out between Oct 16 and Oct 22.
- G hints at a calmer BFCM, so check with Nick that the reveal can back it up.
- Post two of these days apart (B then C works well), and reply to every guess on C.
