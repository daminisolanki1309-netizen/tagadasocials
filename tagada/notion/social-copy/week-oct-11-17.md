## 11. Performance changes when you scale
X · Text post · Product · [Open the calendar card](https://app.notion.com/p/3f3cbbce57df8050b729da6236c2591e)
> Brief: Business and authority angle. A brand's needs evolve as it scales. Shopify is strong, Tagada focuses on performance. No bashing.
**The hook:** the same 2 point drop at two sizes. The reader does the math in their head and lands on their own store.
**Option A (recommended)**
```plain text
At 100 orders a day, losing 2 points of approval rate is a bad afternoon.

At 5,000 orders a day, it's 100 orders gone a day.

The setup that got you to your first million won't carry you to your tenth.
```
**Option B**
```plain text
Your store at $20K a month needs a good theme and a pay button.

Your store at $500K a month needs declined cards retried and a backup when one processor says no.

Shopify nails the first list. We built Tagada for the second.
```
**Option C**
```plain text
Founders plan for more ads and more stock when they scale.

Few plan for checkout and payments turning into someone's full time job.
```
**Sensitivity:** option B names Shopify and describes Tagada. Option A is the safest.
