X · Text post · Educational · For: experienced operators · [Open the calendar card](https://app.notion.com/p/3f3cbbce57df806da8a4da923aca30a7)
> Brief: Pick one specific thing an operator should verify now. Concrete over generic checklist.

V1 (pick)
```plain text
Do this before Black Friday. It takes five minutes.

Open your payments dashboard. Last 30 days. Approved payments divided by attempts. Write the number on a sticky note.

If it drops on the day, you'll know by lunch instead of next Tuesday.
```
V2
```plain text

```
V3
```plain text
One question for this week: if your processor puts your account under review at 9am on Black Friday, what happens at 9:01?

```
V4
```plain text
Pull last month's declines and sort them by reason.

If "Do Not Honor" sits near the top, the decision is coming from your customers' banks. Worth knowing before your biggest traffic week, while you still have time to act on it.
```

```plain text
Every store starts with one job: run the store, meaning products and orders.

Somewhere past $100K a month, a second job shows up. It lives between "add to cart" and "payment approved," and at $500K a month a 2 point drop in approvals is about $10,000.

Most teams never give that job an owner. This carousel is about the brands that do.

Who owns approval rate on your team?
```
