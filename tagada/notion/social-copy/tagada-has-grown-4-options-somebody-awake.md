## What we need from the team before this goes out
- **Names, cities and one true detail** for each person shown (B), with their OK to be featured.
- **Real counts:** people now, people at the start of the year, countries, languages, time zones, open roles.
- **Coverage:** does support really run overnight for US merchants (B slide 6)?
- **Median first-reply time** from support (D slide 4). If it is not good, drop the slide.
- **Hiring:** the actual roles and where the links go.
- **Lease line** (A slide 4): confirm Tagada has no office spend before saying it.

## Why these should work
- Team and people posts beat product posts on company pages by 82% ([LigoSocial, 2,692 posts](https://ligosocial.com/research/linkedin-company-pages-2026)).
- People and places outperform payments content on LinkedIn: a Wise office tour got 16.6x the usual reach ([post](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7487794044912267264/)).
- Hiring posts that play with the format travel: Linear's hiring memo got 270,000+ views ([report](https://letsdatascience.com/news/linear-cofounder-reuses-layoff-template-to-hire-558e1e47)).
- Opening with a stance and then one line per slide is the beehiiv structure from the post you shared.
