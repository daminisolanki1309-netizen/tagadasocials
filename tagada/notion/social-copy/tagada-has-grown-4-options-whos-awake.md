## Design notes
- One real face per person slide, big type, no stock photos. A real, slightly messy team photo beats a polished one.
- Put the roles link in the first comment, not the caption.
- Slide 1 and the last slide matter most. Spend the design time there.

## Confirm before posting
- **Every name, photo, number and role.** Get each person's OK for their photo and the detail under it.
- **Option 3:** Eden's OK, the month of the post, and what really happened next.
- **72% to 83.9%:** the merchant result came from [Tagada's own LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7501672811438960640/). Check it can be repeated.
- **"Always awake" (Option 1):** only true if support really covers every time zone overnight.
- **Risk slide (Option 1):** the risk team signs off on what it says.
- **Jokes with a fact inside** (6 phones, the group chat, the snack): make sure each one is true for the team.
- **Platforms:** no Stripe, PayPal or Shopify named. Eden's original post names one, so Option 3 paraphrases it.
