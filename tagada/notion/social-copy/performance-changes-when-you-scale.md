## The four options
Each is under 280 characters, so it posts as a normal X post. The maths is checked: 2 points is 2 orders at 100 a day and 100 orders at 5,000 a day, which is 50 times the loss for the same dip.
**Option 1. The first million** (keeps the approved opening line, adds the soft CTA)
```plain text
The setup that got you to your first million rarely gets you to your tenth.

Lose 2 points of approvals at 100 orders a day and you lose 2 orders.
At 5,000 a day, you lose 100.

Shopify gets you selling. Tagada keeps the checkout approving as the volume climbs.
```
**Option 2. Same dip** (the multiplier is the hook)
```plain text
A 2 point drop in approvals.

100 orders a day: 2 lost orders.
5,000 orders a day: 100 lost orders.

Same dip, fifty times the bill. Nothing broke. The store just outgrew its setup.

Tagada works on that part: more than one processor behind every payment.
```
**Option 3. Two stores** (the dry, operator-voice one)
```plain text
Store at 100 orders a day: "Approvals dipped 2 points. Annoying."

Same store at 5,000 orders a day: "Approvals dipped 2 points."

That's 100 orders. Every day.

Each point gets more expensive as you grow. Tagada is built for the 5,000 version of your store.
```
**Option 4. What changes** (the most authority-led, closest to the brief)
```plain text
What your checkout needs changes as you grow.

At 100 orders a day, it needs to work.
At 5,000, it needs to keep approving when one processor says no.

Shopify is a strong commerce platform. Tagada's job is the performance side.
```
**My pick:** Option 3 for stopping power (it reads like an operator talking, and the pause before "That's 100 orders" is the whole joke), Option 1 if the team wants to stay closest to the approved draft.
