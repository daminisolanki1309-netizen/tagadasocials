## What I'd run first
- **Static:** 2A, 4B and 1A. Three different reasons to click: urgency with a real deadline, the strongest line, and the plain statement of the problem.
- **Video:** angle 3, as the attention play. Judge it on applications, not views.
- **Swap in** 2B, 1B, 3B and 4A as the second round once you see which angle wins.
---
## Team to confirm before launch
- **Retries on subscriptions (angle 4):** does the automatic retry cover recurring renewals as well as checkout payments? Ad 4A says "declined payments" and works either way, but the angle sells harder if renewals are included. (Tess or Berry)
- **"The second yes" as a name:** it is not on the approved list. It is only a phrase, but it should be agreed once and then used everywhere. (Berry)
- **"Most accounts take a few days":** used in 2A and 2B, taken from the brief. (Berry)
- **Naming:** none of the copy names Stripe, PayPal or Shopify. Keep it that way until the CMO decides.
- **Fictional brand:** keep the "Illustration. Fictional brand." label on the screen and in the description for angle 3.
- **Button:** confirm "Apply Now" is offered for the campaign objective in Ads Manager. If not, use "Learn More" or "Sign Up".
- **Nothing here promises approval.** Do not add "guaranteed" or "instant" anywhere.

## If someone wants a longer primary text, two sourced lines
- Last year payment volume turned up around Nov 17, and fraud ran about 1.7 times normal from Black Friday to Cyber Monday. Source: [Stripe's peak season guide](https://stripe.com/guides/preparing-for-peak-shopping-season-2026).
- Visa's "excessive" level for merchants dropped from 2.2% to 1.5% on April 1, 2026. Source: [Fraud.net](https://fraud.net/resources/visas-vamp-thresholds-drop-to-1-5-on-april-1-are-you-ready).
Both need a team check before they go in an ad.
