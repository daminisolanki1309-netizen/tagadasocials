## How Schwartz would approach it
He would not write about Tagada. He would write to the person he wants to reach: a good operator, engineer or support person who is a little bored where they are and wants to do the best work of their career next to people they respect. That desire already exists. The carousel only has to say "this is the place" without begging. The numbers go in once, early, as proof. The rest is faces, a dry joke and one easy way to say hello.

## Before it goes live
- **\$1B+ processed:** the only public source is Eden's X bio ("\$1B+ processed"). The team should confirm it is all-time volume and approve the wording.
- **50+ people:** confirm it counts everyone, including contractors, so the number can't be challenged in the comments.
- **Countries:** the number is unknown publicly. Fill \[number\] with the real count, or cut it.
- **Faces:** every person in a slide agrees to appear, and their "true detail" is theirs to approve.
- **Promises:** "still answers the DMs", "we read everything", "we find the seat" and "colleagues who reply" are commitments. The team should be ready to keep them before we write them.
- **Not hiring:** keep "not hiring for a specific role today" on the carousel. It reads as honest and still invites people in.
