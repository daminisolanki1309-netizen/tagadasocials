## 20. Peak season exposes weak operations
X · Text post · Educational · [Open the calendar card](https://app.notion.com/p/3f3cbbce57df80cb9353c1476b2fc12b)
> Brief: Strong take: peak season exposes weak operations. One concrete reason higher traffic reveals what normal volume hides. Concise, operator-first.
**The hook:** an uncomfortable line every operator recognizes (the thing you fix by hand), then the math that turns it into a Black Friday problem.
**Option A (recommended)**
```plain text
Black Friday finds the thing you've been fixing by hand.

A bug that hits 1 in 100 orders is one support ticket a day in October. Someone sorts it out and moves on.

At 30x the orders, it's 30 tickets a day, and that someone is busy.
```
**Option B**
```plain text
Fraud runs 1.7x higher over BFCM (Stripe), so stores tighten their fraud rules in a panic.

The rule that blocked 3 bad orders a week in October can turn away hundreds of real buyers on Black Friday. Your fraud filter can cost you more than the fraud.
```
**Option C**
```plain text
Any process that depends on one person remembering will break on Black Friday.

That person will be busy.
```
