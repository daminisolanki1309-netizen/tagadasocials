**Design note for all three:** big type, one idea per slide, and a small tag above the cover line that reads TAGADA HAS GROWN.

## Option A: The job ad with no job (pick, most personality)
**Why it stops the scroll:** a job listing that admits it has no job is a pattern break. It copies the Elisabetta Torretti hiring-post move (a parody of a boring format, built on who the writer really is) and the Linear hiring-memo move (270K+ views). It filters for people with a sense of humour, which is the people you want.

## Option C: Proof first (most credible, least playful)
**Why it works:** it earns trust from merchants and serious candidates in the same swipe. Showing the refunds next to the revenue is the Bencivenga move: say the less flattering number yourself and the rest becomes believable.

The \$1.12M and \$22K figures come from Tagada's Instagram proof post, so confirm with the team that both merchants agree to be quoted. Slide 4 is a team claim, so confirm it reads true to them.

## What I need from the team for any of these
- The real numbers: people, countries, merchants.
- One true, specific detail per person who appears (the thing they always have open in a tab, the message they're known for).
- The place people should reach out: a DM to a named person or an email address.
- Yes or no on being open about pay (Option A, slide 6).
