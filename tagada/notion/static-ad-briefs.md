**Rules every brief follows** (from the production pack, Oct 5): no promise of approval, no "no more bans", no price or fee, no number of processors, no figure without a source, "can" on every retry claim, never "smart routing" or "instant". Captions are 125 characters max and headlines 40 max (checked for every brief). Nick approves every text before it goes live. Square 1080 × 1080.

**The angles**
- **Shut down before:** name what they lived, answer with flow, never with "no more bans".
- **Single point of failure:** one payment account carries the whole store.
- **Peak-season readiness:** Black Friday is the worst week to find a gap.
- **Revenue leaking quietly:** declines and failed renewals that nobody retries.
- **Simplicity:** one processor to connect, the acquiring arranged behind it.
- **The missing layer:** resellers sell one slice; merchants buy the rest elsewhere.
- **Don't build it:** dev shops plug it in and resell it.
- **Selectivity:** Rx is by application only, from \$200K a month.
- **Stack fatigue:** five logins, five bills, one store.

## 4. How to read a brief
Open any row in the database below. Each row has: the format, the angle, the hook, the on-image copy (what the designer puts on the square), the caption (125 max) and headline (40 max), the CTA button and the one it is tested against, two alternative hooks for the hook test, the reference ad with its link, why the reference works, the claims check, and the ad name. Status starts at "Draft for Tess".
## 5. Hook and CTA tests
**How every test runs**
1. Change one thing only. Same image, same caption, same headline, same CTA. Only the hook moves (or only the button).
2. Hook first, CTA second. A button only matters once people stop, so the CTA test runs on the winning hook.
3. Name the files so the test reads itself: V1 is hook A, V2 hook B, V3 hook C (for example `FixBefore_NotesApp_NextHold_V2`).
4. Read at the pack's thresholds: attention after 5,000 impressions per version; lead to application after 30 CRM leads; Partners on calls worth having. Decide on the second Monday read.
