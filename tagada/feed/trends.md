Videos already posted elsewhere count if they are the creator's own.
- Platforms: X
- Numbers: Social Media Today (Nov 1): 15 videos on 15 days, vertical, under three minutes; follows X's $1M livestream fund in July. X's own help page returned 403, so the bonus amount and dates are not confirmed here.
