- Evidence: Forter launched an "Agentic Commerce Engine" on Oct 13 and says agentic orders on its network are up more than 4x since August while automated fraud doubled in two months; under 11% of small merchants are ready (Forkast, Oct 18). TechCrunch (Sep 28): a major ecommerce platform opened checkout to browser-based AI agents. On X, @coinbase pitched trading "from Claude, ChatGPT, Grok" (Oct 20) and @NotionHQ launched more capable agents (Oct 20).
