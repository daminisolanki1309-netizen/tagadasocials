## Culture

### The Jimmy Dean harmonica ad
- What it is: A 15-second Jimmy Dean breakfast sandwich ad from 2018 ("Warm It Up"), built on the harmonica from Jimmy Dean's "The Cajun Queen", became TikTok's new brainrot sound. People loop the opening, swap in other faces, play it on a calculator, or just point out how loud the harmonica is. Know Your Meme added the entry on Oct 29. We found no response from the brand.
- Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube
- Numbers: Know Your Meme (Oct 29): the original ad has 25M+ YouTube views; the first known remix by @grantrimi went up Oct 22. ## Business

### The FTC goes after the price at the register
- What it is: Two FTC moves in three days, both about whether the price a shopper sees is the price they pay. On Oct 26 the FTC and 20 states sued Dollar General, saying thousands of stores charged more at the register than the shelf tag or ad, despite "tens of thousands" of complaints. On Oct 28 the FTC sent orders to Walmart, Target, Delta, Uber, DoorDash and others asking how personal data shapes the prices people see. Dollar General disputes it and says it wants "100% accuracy between shelf and register prices". The orders are a request for documents, not an accusation.
- Platforms: TikTok, X, LinkedIn, news
- Numbers: CNBC (Oct 26, updated Oct 28): 20 states, "tens of thousands" of complaints, shares down about 4% after the news. Fox Business (Oct 27): the FTC's consumer protection director said the company "betrayed the trust of millions of American consumers." CBS (updated Oct 29): orders sent on Wednesday, Oct 28; the comment period on the FTC's proposed personalized-pricing disclosure rule runs to Nov 21. TikTok via Playwright: the "dollar general lawsuit" discover page listed 16 videos posted Oct 28 to 31; @nbcnews 37.4K plays; @eastalabamanow "Shelf says one price. Checkout says another?" 21.9K plays, 414 shares on 6,314 followers (Oct 28).
- Examples (opened in this run):
  - https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/ftc-dollar-general-lawsuit.html (the lawsuit, the 20 states, Dollar General's response)
  - https://www.cbsnews.com/atlanta/news/ftc-investigation-delta-walmart-personal-data-prices-algorithms/ (the Oct 28 orders, the Nov 21 comment deadline)
  - https://www.tiktok.com/@eastalabamanow/video/7701511281102048525 ("Shelf says one price. Checkout says another?", 21.9K plays, Oct 28)

- Angle: Tagada on LinkedIn or X, a plain explainer that names no retailer: "The FTC spent this week on one question: is the price a shopper sees the price they pay? On a Tagada checkout, the price on the product page is the price on the order confirmation. Every line in between has a name: [shipping], [tax], [upsell they added]." Keep it calm, no villain framing.
- Shelf life: 2 to 4 weeks (Nov 21 comment deadline, more filings likely)

### DoorDash signs for Instacart, $17B (Still rising)
- What it is: Last week's talks became a signed deal on Oct 29: DoorDash will buy Instacart for $65 a share, about $17B, in cash and stock. It keeps the Instacart brand, app and enterprise tech. It needs approval and is not expected to close until late 2027 or early 2028, and analysts expect antitrust questions.

TechCrunch (Oct 29): Instacart market cap above $13B; Tony Xu: "Grocery is one of the hardest things to get right online." Also covered by Bloomberg, ABC, Fast Company, AdExchanger and Campaign.

### Meta shows which asset in an ad did the work
- What it is: Meta Ads Manager added a "Breakdown" view that shows how each image, video and text variation in an ad performed on its own. Carousel and collection formats are left out, and some custom conversions such as "Subscribe" get no per-asset view. It lands just as brands lock BFCM creative.
- Platforms: Facebook, Instagram
- Numbers: Social Media Today (Oct 27): the Breakdown covers single media (images and videos) and text variations; it excludes "Show as carousel" and "Show as collection" and gives no insight for "Donate" and "Subscribe" conversions. No Meta performance numbers were given.
- Examples (opened in this run):
  - https://socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-adds-individual-performance-data-for-ad-creative/872981 (what the Breakdown shows and its limits)
- Momentum: New
- Angle: Tagada on LinkedIn, for operators planning BFCM: "Meta now tells you which image got the click. Your checkout tells you which image got the order. Put the two side by side before you scale a winner for Black Friday on Nov 27." No claims about Tagada reporting features unless confirmed.
- Shelf life: 3 to 4 weeks, through BFCM
