### The FTC sues a payment processor over "save 40 to 60%" and a $695 exit fee
- What it is: The FTC and Washington State sued Riverside Payments and its affiliates, which settled for $600,000. The complaint says Riverside promised small businesses 40% to 60% savings for switching processors, left activation, batch and compliance fees out of quotes, buried a three-year auto-renewal and a $695 early termination fee in a 26-page e-agreement, and advertised a "no-risk" cancellation guarantee that merchants could not use. It is the second processor fee case in a week, after the FTC and eight states sued Toast (Oct 31).
- Platforms: News, payments trade press
- Numbers: Payments Dive (Nov 4): $600,000 settlement; 40% to 60% savings claims; $695 early termination fee; 26-page agreement; agent earnings claims of $75,000 to $95,000. Social pickup was not measured this run (no X or LinkedIn search without a login).
- Examples (opened in this run, curl):
  - https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/payment-processor-settle-ftc-alleged-deceptive-fees/873669/ (Nov 4, HTTP 200)
- Momentum: New
- Angle: TagadaPay on X and LinkedIn (Marcus), as a two-line mechanic, not a news explainer: "The FTC just settled with a processor over a $695 early termination fee buried in a 26-page agreement.
