Open the sandbox The terms that decide it The questions that decide the choice of a processor in our categories, answered as they are written in the agreement you sign. Minimum monthly volume None. No monthly minimum, no minimum fee, and your rate does not depend on your volume. The volume you declare when you apply sizes our review; it is not a commitment. Rolling reserve Usually 10% of each sale, held 60 to 90 days, then released automatically. It can be lower or zero depending on your history and category, and the exact figure is in your pricing agreement before you sign. If chargebacks or alerts spike, our risk system can raise it up to 40%. Anything higher is decided by a person. Every change is sent to you in writing with the reason, and you have 30 days to ask for a review. Payout timing Payouts run every day. A sale becomes payable once it clears with the card networks: 2 days after capture (T+2) in the US, Canada, the UK, the EU and Switzerland. It takes longer in some markets (T+7 in Hong Kong) and on some high-risk contracts, and your contract states it. Each daily run sends your available balance, less reserve and any debt, in your settlement currency. Weekends and bank holidays can add a day or two. Chargebacks $25 per dispute and $30 per chargeback alert, unless your agreement states otherwise. We charge no fee that is not in your schedule, apart from card-network fines passed on at cost. You can contest a dispute with evidence from your dashboard. The card issuer decides, and an escalated case is settled under the card network's rules, not by us. Suspension We can stop processing or payouts at once for illegal activity, prohibited products, selling outside what you were approved for, a breach of the agreement, an unanswered information request, or when an acquirer or card network requires it. On our side a person makes that decision, not an automated system, and we tell you what to fix and by when to resume. Leaving Either side can end the contract with 30 days' written notice. Payments already made keep settling. Your remaining balance and reserve are held for up to 180 days after the contract ends, longer only while a chargeback or claim is still open, and then paid out less any debt.
