## Seven patterns she rejects

1. **Fear and villain posts we made up.** Payout holds, "180 days", "3 to 5 business days", "We're reviewing your account, Friday 11:04pm", "It's only one processor, what could go wrong", risk models watching you, debanking, villain walk-up reels. 20+ of the Oct 18 to 20 skips. The avatars say Marcus does stop for hold stories, but real ones with real numbers. A brand inventing the scary scene reads as fear-mongering.
2. **Meme or sound + "but for your checkout".** Randy Marsh vs., Stop the Wedding, Biddy Blas, Black Cat Clapping, Thank You Babeh, Tip Toein', the stolen-package snowclone, McRib vs Bitcoin, the Housewife sound, abandoned-cart flirts, the post-purchase pickup line, the obituary carousel, "I'm so sorry" reveal. The bridge to checkout is forced and the joke is borrowed, not ours.
3. **News + "what this means for your store".** Meta product browsing, Walmart+ bundle, Threads passing X, OCC stablecoin rule, PHP2Shell, Etsy AI rule, AliExpress on YouTube, round-number pricing. A consultant's newsletter, not a banger.
4. **Corporate self-talk.** "We raised $20M from our own customers", "What a night with our merchants", field reports, "Some of you waited weeks, that's on us", a hiring post rewritten, The Table recap in counts, "TagadaRx is open, limited spots", "Tagada is how the French say ta-da", "gm".
5. **Consultant checklists.** "Before you announce a drop time: four checks", "Write your outage message before you need it", "October is the last calm month", a decline-code video series, a whiteboard series part 1. Useful, never exciting.
6. **Countdowns and urgency.** "Time to lock in before Black Friday", BFCM countdowns.
7. **Results and numbers we don't have.** "One merchant, 30 days, $1.12M", invented reviews of our own product.

## The bar from now on

A swipe ships only if it passes all five:

1. **It is an execution, not a caption.** Say what we film or build: the shot, the stunt, the visual, the first frame. If it only works as text, it must be a one-liner an operator would screenshot.
2. **One avatar, in their world.** Dylan: X and Reels, screen recordings, revenue screenshots, tool teardowns, speed ("live tonight"). Marcus: LinkedIn and X, real hold stories with numbers, published terms, approval rate by MID, a named person who picks up. Brooke: Instagram and LinkedIn, women founders with real numbers, compliance in plain English, behind the scenes of building a clinic, never "printing" or "scaling" next to weight loss.
3. **Only we could post it.** Our team, our product on screen, our merchants (with permission), our founders. The remove-the-logo test.
4. **The idea is ours, not borrowed.** A trend can be the vehicle (a sound, a format), but the joke or the twist has to come from the operator's real life, not "X but for checkout".
5. **Proof is shown, not claimed.** Screen, document, person, number we can source. No made-up scene of a frozen account.
